Excavation & Piling

The foundation system, a massive reinforced concrete mat supported by high-capacity bored reinforced concrete piles, was initiated. This phase required innovative concrete mixes to withstand the sulfate-rich groundwater.

Level 110 Spire Jacking

The central core reached Level 110. A revolutionary "buttressed core" design was utilized to manage lateral wind loads. High-pressure concrete pumping reached record heights, overcoming extreme temperature and pressure hurdles.

Installation of the world's highest aluminum and glass facade was completed. Over 26,000 glass panels were individually hand-cut to ensure aerodynamic precision, designed to withstand the desert's extreme heat and wind.
